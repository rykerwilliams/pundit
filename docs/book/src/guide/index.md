# Using pundit

pundit is built around one way of working: open the match, watch it, and talk
over the moments that matter. Every page here is one part of that, and they are
in the order you meet them.

1. [Installing](install.md) — the package, what your machine needs, and where
   the app keeps its files.
2. [Projects and matches](projects.md) — starting a match from its video files,
   opening one you made earlier, and what is inside the folder.
3. [Watching the game](watching.md) — playing, skipping, scanning fast, zooming
   and fitting the window.
4. [Recording commentary](recording.md) — talking over the game, drawing on it,
   and the webcam or photo in the corner.
5. [Clips](clips.md) — naming, tagging, previewing and tidying what you
   recorded.
6. [The scoreboard and match clock](match.md) — teams, goals and periods, typed
   in or tagged live.
7. [Slates](slates.md) — marking a range now and recording over it later.
8. [Player highlights](highlights.md) — a ring that follows a player.
9. [Transcripts](transcripts.md) — the words of a clip, written out on your own
   machine.
10. [Exporting](exporting.md) — clips, reels and the whole match as `.mp4`, and
    the files that land beside them.
11. [The basket](basket.md) — one film from clips in several matches.
12. [When something goes wrong](troubleshooting.md) — where the log is and what
    to look for.

The [keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) page lists every key the app listens for,
and what stops each one working.
