# Watching the game

The videos in a match play as one timeline: the readout and the scrubber count
them as one game, and playback carries straight on across the join.

## Playing and skipping

- <kbd>Space</kbd> plays and pauses.
- <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> (or <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>) skip 3 seconds;
  with <kbd>Shift</kbd>, 10.
- <kbd>,</kbd> and <kbd>.</kbd> step one frame back or forward while paused,
  and the readout shows tenths of a second.
- <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> jump to the previous or next goal or period
  mark.
- **The mouse wheel scrubs**, over the picture or the scrubber: a notch is 3
  seconds, <kbd>Shift</kbd> makes it 10, and rolling away from you goes
  forward. It keeps working while you record, where dragging the scrubber
  does not.

## Scanning fast

While playing, <kbd>L</kbd> speeds up through 2×, 4×, 8×, 16× and 32×, and
<kbd>J</kbd> slows down again; the speed button beside Play does the same.
Every frame is still decoded at 32×, so what you see is the game, not key frames
flashing past. The sound is muted above 1×, and any pause puts you back to 1× on
the frame you were looking at.

## Zooming and panning

- <kbd>3</kbd> zooms in and <kbd>2</kbd> zooms out, towards the pointer when it
  is over the picture. <kbd>1</kbd> goes back to the whole picture.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + wheel (or a two-finger scroll) zooms about the pointer.
- Once zoomed in, a click-drag pans.

Zoom is part of what a recording captures, so zooming while you talk replays in
the clip and its export.

## The scoreboard on the picture

Once the [teams are set up](match.md), the clock and score are drawn in the top
left as you scan — by the same code that burns them into an export, so what you
see is what you will get. The board hides while you drag the scrubber and comes
back when you let go.

## Making room

- **Fit** (<kbd>F</kbd>) closes the window up around the picture so the black
  bars go. It only ever makes the window smaller, and never redraws the picture
  at a different size. Leave full screen first.
- **The side panels** have a grip either side of the video: drag it to widen the
  sidebar or the inspector. The widths are remembered on this machine.
- **Volume** sets how loud the game is while you watch. It does not change what
  is exported.
