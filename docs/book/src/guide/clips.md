# Clips

Every recording is a clip. Select one in the **Clips** list and the inspector
shows what you can change about it.

## Finding them again

- **Name** and **Notes** are yours to write.
- **Tags** are comma separated, and offer the tags you have already used as you
  type.
- **Filter by tag** narrows the list to the clips with one tag.
- Drag clips to reorder them, or **Sort by position** to put them in game order.
  The order is the order they export in.

## Playing one back

**Preview clip** plays a clip as it will export — your voice, your drawings, the
inset and the scoreboard, all in step with the game. **Jump to clip start** takes
the game video to where the clip begins, without playing the commentary.

## Tidying up

- <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or **Delete clip**, deletes the selected clip. Its
  recording goes to a trash folder inside the project, and
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> puts it back where it was.
- <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>
  undo and redo every change to a clip.
- Right-click a clip for its menu, which also has **Add to basket** — see
  [the basket](basket.md).
